# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PrepWise serves job candidates preparing resumes, job-description matching, interviews, and follow-up practice. Users are usually in a focused editing or review workflow and need fast, trustworthy feedback while they compare resume content against role requirements.

## Product Purpose

PrepWise helps candidates complete the loop from resume editing to JD analysis, simulated interviews, review, and targeted improvement. Success means users can edit structured resume content, see accurate real-time template previews, export polished resumes, and continue into interview preparation without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Precise, supportive, professional. The interface should feel like a capable career-productivity workspace: calm enough for long editing sessions, opinionated enough to guide better resumes, and clear enough that users trust the preview before exporting.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative UI that distracts from editing, deeply nested card stacks, inconsistent form controls, template previews that ignore editor settings, weak contrast, and resume layouts where style controls do not match exported content.

## Design Principles

- Make editing and preview behavior match: typography, spacing, visibility, and content order should stay consistent across the left editor, right preview, export, and template switching.
- Keep the workbench scannable: reduce nested surfaces, make module hierarchy clear, and reserve emphasis for actions and state.
- Preserve resume credibility: template styling should support professional reading, not decoration.
- Prefer predictable controls: fonts, colors, spacing, module visibility, and custom template options should use one consistent control vocabulary.
- Verify visually before claiming UI work is complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for practical WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and readable Chinese/English mixed typography across editor controls and resume previews.
